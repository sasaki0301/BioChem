Lesson 2: Chemical Basis of Life

All living things are made of chemicals that interact in precise and coordinated ways. Understanding the elements and molecules of life helps explain how organisms grow, reproduce, and maintain stability.

1. Elements Essential for Life

Life on Earth depends on a small group of chemical elements. Out of more than 100 known elements, only a few make up most living matter.

  • Carbon (C): Backbone of all organic molecules.
  • Hydrogen (H): Found in water and organic compounds.
  • Oxygen (O): Needed for respiration and metabolism.
  • Nitrogen (N): Key element in amino acids and nucleic acids.
  • Phosphorus (P): Component of ATP and DNA.
  • Sulfur (S): Found in some proteins.
These six elements — C, H, O, N, P, and S — are often called the “biogenic elements” because they are vital for life.

The major elements of life make up most of our body’s mass and are found in biomolecules.

2. Chemical Bonds and Molecules

Atoms form chemical bonds to create molecules. The type of bonding determines the structure and behavior of biological molecules.

Bond TypeDescriptionExample
CovalentAtoms share electrons; very strong bonds.Water (H₂O), glucose
IonicAtoms transfer electrons; forms ions.Na⁺ + Cl⁻ → NaCl
Hydrogen BondsWeak attractions between polar molecules.Between water molecules or DNA bases

3. Water: The Molecule of Life

Water makes up 60–90% of living organisms. Its unique properties make it indispensable for life.

  • Polarity: Water molecules have partial charges that enable hydrogen bonding.
  • Cohesion and Adhesion: Water sticks to itself and other surfaces.
  • Temperature Regulation: High specific heat stabilizes body temperature.
  • Universal Solvent: Many molecules dissolve in water, enabling biochemical reactions.

Polarity allows water molecules to form hydrogen bonds, essential for many life processes.

5. Key Terms Summary

  • Biogenic Elements: The six essential elements of life — C, H, O, N, P, S.
  • Covalent Bond: A strong bond formed by shared electrons.
  • Ionic Bond: Bond between charged particles (ions).
  • Hydrogen Bond: Weak bond between polar molecules like water.
  • Polarity: Unequal distribution of charge in a molecule.
