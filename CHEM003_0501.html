Big Idea: Amino acids are the building blocks of proteins and are essential for structure, function, and metabolism in living organisms.

1. General Structure of Amino Acids

All amino acids share a common structure centered on a carbon atom (α-carbon).

  • Amino group (−NH₂)
  • Carboxyl group (−COOH)
  • Hydrogen atom
  • R-group (side chain)

General structure of an amino acid.

Important: The R-group determines the identity and properties of each amino acid.

3. Zwitterions and Acid-Base Behavior

Amino acids can act as both acids and bases. In water, they often exist as zwitterions.

Zwitterion: Molecule with both positive and negative charges
  • −NH₂ becomes −NH₃⁺
  • −COOH becomes −COO⁻
Quick Check: Why are amino acids amphoteric?
Because they can donate and accept protons.
