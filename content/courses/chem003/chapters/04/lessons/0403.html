Big Idea: Complex lipids are essential for building cell membranes and enabling life at the cellular level.

1. What Are Complex Lipids?

Complex lipids are lipids that contain additional functional groups beyond fatty acids and glycerol.

TypeAdditional GroupFunction
PhospholipidsPhosphateCell membrane structure
GlycolipidsCarbohydrateCell recognition
LipoproteinsProteinLipid transport
Quick Check: What makes complex lipids different from simple lipids?
They contain additional functional groups such as phosphate, carbohydrate, or protein.

2. Phospholipids

Phospholipids are the main structural components of cell membranes.

  • Hydrophilic head (phosphate group)
  • Hydrophobic tails (fatty acids)

Amphipathic nature allows membrane formation.

Important: Because phospholipids are amphipathic, they self-assemble into bilayers in water.
Quick Check: Why do phospholipids form bilayers?
Hydrophilic heads face water, while hydrophobic tails avoid water, forming a bilayer.

3. Glycolipids

Glycolipids contain carbohydrate groups and are found on the outer surface of membranes.

  • Involved in cell recognition
  • Help in immune response
  • Important in cell signaling
Quick Check: Where are glycolipids located?
On the outer surface of the cell membrane.

4. Lipoproteins

Lipoproteins transport lipids through the bloodstream.

TypeFunction
HDLRemoves cholesterol
LDLDelivers cholesterol
Quick Check: Which is considered “good cholesterol”?
HDL is considered good cholesterol.

5. Structure of Biological Membranes

Cell membranes are composed mainly of a phospholipid bilayer with embedded proteins.

  • Phospholipid bilayer
  • Proteins embedded
  • Cholesterol regulates fluidity
Fluid Mosaic Model: Membrane is fluid and proteins move within it.
Quick Check: Why is it called fluid mosaic?
Because the membrane is fluid and composed of many different components.

6. Membrane Functions

  • Barrier between inside and outside
  • Regulates transport
  • Cell communication
  • Structural support
Quick Check: What is the main role of membranes?
To regulate what enters and leaves the cell.

7. Transport Across Membranes

TypeDescription
DiffusionMovement from high to low concentration
Facilitated diffusionUses proteins
Active transportRequires energy
Quick Check: Which requires energy?
Active transport requires energy.
